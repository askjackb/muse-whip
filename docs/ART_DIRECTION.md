# Art direction contract

**Confirmed:** primarily Castlevania, dark gothic horror, highly detailed HD/modernized presentation, and no visible pixels. **Proposed execution:** painted 2D illustrations with layered scenery, controlled atmospheric lighting, and depth cues. Full animation is mandatory; the precise style pack and character design still need calibration.

## Visual grammar

An original hunter: recognizable warrior silhouette, readable face/hair mass, leather/metal separation, coherent original costume construction, and a whip that reads at gameplay scale. The hunter's costume is an original design fixed by the P2 style pack ([ART_BIBLE.md](ART_BIBLE.md)); do not derive it from any franchise character or average incompatible references across generations.

World: ruined masonry, pointed arches, funerary carvings, dead vegetation, corroded iron, mist, and evidence of abandonment. Use large readable structures before microdetail. Dense texture is allowed in quiet areas; traversable edges and enemy silhouettes get clean separation.

Proposed palette roles: near-black violet recesses; cold slate stone; desaturated blue-green distance; restrained bone highlights; scarce ember/ochre for interactables. These are roles, not a mandatory finished palette. Avoid making the whole image equally dark or equally detailed.

Proposed light: consistent broad upper-left form light in baked illustrations, soft occlusion, transparent sprites without floor shadows. Local scene lights may add mood but must not be necessary to identify threats. Mirrored sprites must remain plausible; use broad highlights rather than strong one-sided cast shadows. Enemy projectiles need a clear shape as well as color contrast.

Depth: distant low-contrast silhouettes, midground architecture, sharply readable play plane, sparse foreground framing. Fog must not cover attack tells or platform edges. The default is a true 2D side view with parallax layers; 3D dioramas and perspective cameras are not implied by the Octopath reference.

## What “HD” means here

Crisp, deliberate shapes at the expected display size, coherent materials, controlled detail, stable animation anatomy, and clean alpha edges. It does not mean arbitrary 4K files for every sprite. No visible pixel grid, nearest-neighbor pixel styling, or pixel-art character sprites. Octopath informs depth and atmosphere only.

## Calibration before production

The future art task first produces a small, explicitly authorized style pack: hero neutral pose; one attack key pose; one enemy; a 3×3 terrain patch; one layered environment mockup at the logical gameplay view. Place the character at contract scale. Review at full logical view and phone display size.

Select one coherent pack as the canonical reference and assign a style-lock revision. Only then derive animation and environment variants. Record reference paths/IDs, tool/model/version when exposed, prompt, date, and revision. Unknown model or seed values must remain unknown.

## Reject

Photorealistic backgrounds mixed accidentally with anime sprites; chibi proportions; decorative detail that resembles a hazard; frame-to-frame changes in face, costume, limb length, or weapon; arbitrary bloom; visual noise along collision edges; baked UI/text; isometric perspective; fog hiding enemies; bright whimsical scenery inconsistent with D04.

Castlevania is the primary design reference for tone and mechanics only. Do not reproduce franchise characters, costumes, names, or world elements, and do not introduce recognizable Mario content or an expanded crossover roster without a later scope change.
